# Content license

Everything under `worlds/` — maps, episodes, dialogue and other copy, and all
art (building facades, character sheets, portraits, tiles, palettes) — plus
all art contributed to this project, is licensed under **Creative Commons
Attribution 4.0 International (CC BY 4.0)**.

In plain words: **anyone may copy, share, adapt and use the work, including
commercially, as long as they credit the creator.**

- Human-readable summary: <https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/>
- Full legal code: <https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/legalcode>

(The code that makes the game run — everything under `engine/`, `scripts/`,
`studio/`, and the rest of the repository outside `worlds/` — is licensed
separately under Apache 2.0; see `LICENSE`.)

## Attribution

Contributors keep the copyright to what they make. Credit is given in-game
("painted by ___" on a building, or the equivalent for other contributions)
and recorded in each world's `credits.json`. That in-game and in-repo credit
*is* the attribution CC BY 4.0 requires — there is no separate paperwork to
sign.
